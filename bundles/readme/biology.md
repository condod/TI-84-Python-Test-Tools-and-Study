# Biology & Lab Science Bundle — TI-84 Plus CE Python

6 standalone TI-84 Plus CE **Python Edition** programs for introductory Biology,
Genetics, Ecology, and the solution-prep arithmetic every wet lab runs on.

## What's Included

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `punnett_square_solver.py` | Monohybrid or dihybrid cross calculator: enter each parent's genotype and get every offspring genotype and phenotype with their ratios — the classic 1:2:1 / 3:1 monohybrid and 9:3:3:1 dihybrid results fall straight out. |
| `hardy_weinberg.py` | Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium from an allele frequency, from the recessive phenotype frequency (`q²`, the usual exam wording), or from observed genotype counts. Gives `p`, `q`, the `p²`/`2pq`/`q²` frequencies, carrier frequency, and expected counts — and the counts path runs a chi-square test of whether the population is actually in equilibrium. |
| `chi_square_genetics.py` | Chi-square goodness-of-fit against 3:1, 9:3:3:1, 1:1, 1:2:1 or a custom ratio, with a per-category table, degrees of freedom, and built-in critical values at the 0.05 and 0.01 levels. |
| `population_growth.py` | Exponential `N = N0·e^(rt)` with doubling time (or half-life), and logistic growth with percentage of carrying capacity, instantaneous `dN/dt`, and the inflection point at `K/2`. Refuses targets at or above `K` with an explanation. |
| `dilution_calculator.py` | `C1V1 = C2V2` solved for any variable, the diluent to add and the fold-dilution, dilution factors from volumes or concentrations, full serial-dilution plans, and molarity ↔ moles ↔ grams. |
| `surface_area_volume.py` | Surface area, volume and the SA:V ratio for spheres, cubes, cylinders and boxes, how scaling every length by `k` changes each, Kleiber's-law metabolic rate from body mass, and diffusion time from distance. |

`chi_square_genetics.py` also ships in the Statistics, Probability & Discrete Math
bundle — if you buy both, it is the same file.

**Course fit:** Intro Biology, Genetics, Ecology, Cell Biology, and Lab Methods.

**One thing to know:** College Board has announced that from 2027, AP® Biology and AP®
Environmental Science will not permit handheld calculators with storage capabilities.
Treat these as coursework, lab and homework tools, and read the exam-policy section below.
