# Chemistry & Exam Tools Bundle — TI-84 Plus CE Python

7 standalone TI-84 Plus CE **Python Edition** programs for General and Introductory
Chemistry, plus two study-workflow tools that are useful in any STEM course.

## What's Included

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `ideal_gas_law.py` | Ideal Gas Law solver (enter any three of P, V, n, T) and a Combined Gas Law solver taking five of the six state-1/state-2 values. |
| `stoichiometry_molar_mass.py` | Molar mass from element symbol/count pairs using a built-in element table, then mass ↔ moles conversion with that molar mass or one you supply. |
| `acid_base_calculator.py` | pH and pOH from `[H+]` or `[OH-]` and back again, plus buffer pH by the Henderson-Hasselbalch equation, with an acidic/basic/neutral classification. |
| `reaction_kinetics.py` | Zero-, first- and second-order kinetics for a single reactant: half-life, `[A]` at a given time by the integrated rate law, and the time to reach a target `[A]`. |
| `unit_converter.py` | Length, mass, pressure, temperature and energy conversions from a numbered menu. |
| `formula_flashcards.py` | Self-quiz formula recall drill by subject with self-graded scoring. **A memorisation aid for self-study — not an answer-lookup tool.** |
| `exam_countdown_drill.py` | Countdown timer plus a random mental-math drill (arithmetic, order-of-magnitude estimation, percentages) that checks your typed answer. |

**Course fit:** General Chemistry I/II, Introductory Chemistry, and practice-exam time
management for any STEM course.
